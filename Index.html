Why students choose the Balkans

Affordable living

Rent, food and transport cost less than in many EU capitals, so your budget goes further.

English-taught programs

Many universities offer degrees taught in English, especially in medicine, engineering and business.

Central location

Easy travel to the rest of Europe, with rich history, coastlines and mountains on your doorstep.

About Us

Study in Balkans is an education guidance project that connects international students with universities in Southeast Europe.

Our mission

To make studying abroad simple, honest and affordable. We give clear information so that students and parents can decide with confidence.

Our vision

To become the most trusted guide for students looking at the Balkan region for higher education.
